FAQs

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major payment cards including Visa, Mastercard and American Express. Customers can also pay via invoice. Contact our team today to get started.

Is it possible to move my subscription to someone else in my organization?

Yes, the license administrator for your organization can set up subscriptions and manage users through the Minitab License Portal.

What are Simul8's system requirements?

Take a look at our system requirements page for an overview of the computer specification requirements for Simul8.

Can I manage Simul8 alongside my other Minitab products?

Yes — everything is handled through the Minitab License Portal, giving you a single place to access, manage, and administer all your Minitab solutions. You can learn more about installation and deployment of Minitab solutions here.

Do I get OptQuest with my subscription?

All our subscriptions are eligible for the OptQuest integration. To purchase OptQuest for your subscription please contact us or you can ask your account manager for more information.
